Every location has one of three types

One list drives the locations section, the menu, the booking picker, the footer and the map. Changing a location from Teaser to Bookable is a one-field edit.

Bookable

Open Zagreb centre

Example: Zagreb Zapad today.

Shows: photo, address, hours, map, gallery, “Rezerviraj” button
Appears in: locations section, Centar field in booking step 1, contact, map, footer
Rezerviraj goes to: booking step 1 with this centre prefilled
name: Zagreb Zapad
type: bookable
slug: zagreb-zapad
address, hours, phone, photos
Teaser

Coming soon

Example: Zagreb Istok, Zagreb Jug while in preparation.

Shows: name, area, “Uskoro” + opening month; no booking
Appears in: locations section (greyed), Centar field as “uskoro” (not selectable), footer
Rezerviraj: disabled. Optional later: “Obavijesti me” e-mail sign-up
name: Zagreb Istok
type: teaser
openingLabel: [MJESEC GODINA]
area or address (optional)
External

Other franchise

Example: Wien, Graz Puntigam, Graz City, Stuttgart.

Shows: city + small photo in the brand strip “5 centara u 3 zemlje”
Appears in: brand strip and footer only — never in booking, menu or SEO text
Click goes to: that franchise’s own website, new tab
name: Wien
type: external
externalUrl: [skintastic.at]
country: AT
Booking button rule Every “Rezerviraj” opens booking step 1 directly. Step 1 has a Centar field: filled automatically with 1 bookable centre or from a location card; otherwise the visitor picks it there.
